About

Keys first. Everything else follows.

Daniel Slugoski is a Connecticut-based musician, keyboardist and pianist who spends his time between the stage and the studio. He performs live with bands and solo, covering everything from full-band keys rigs to solo piano sets.

Alongside live work, he writes and records original music, produces music videos, and collaborates with other musicians on records, sessions and creative projects. The throughline is a classical piano foundation pushed into modern, contemporary sound.
